## Full description

Perfect Drop is a fast third-person stacking challenge where every drop matters.

Time the moving block, preserve your platform and climb a 30-stage tower. Clean landings build your score, Perfect drops grow your combo, and a miss ends the run. Reach the crown, collect Coins, unlock visual themes and immediately start the next tower.

Features:
- One-input timing gameplay
- 30-stage tower runs
- Perfect / Good / Miss feedback
- Personal bests and persistent progression
- Daily reward and daily challenge
- Cosmetic block and arena themes
- Fast retry and next-tower flow
- Touch, keyboard/mouse and controller bindings
- Reduced-motion and audio controls

Purchases are optional. Paid items do not directly buy leaderboard score.
